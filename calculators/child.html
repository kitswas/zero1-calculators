The baby decision framework

Do we want a child, or are we trying to satisfy family expectations? Are we delaying because of fear, or because our system is not ready? If we have a child, whose life changes the most?

Plan these five systems beforehand:

  • Care system — Who takes care of the child?
  • Money system — Can we afford it without stress?
  • Career system — Whose career adjusts, and how?
  • Emergency system — What if something goes wrong?
  • Support system — Family, friends, or paid help?

“Having a child is not a bad financial decision. But pretending that love alone will handle the cost is dangerous.”
